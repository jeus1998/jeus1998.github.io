  - ***hoverable*** 
| 부모에서 전달 | 자식 hoverable 값 |
| --- | --- |
| `<list-table hoverable />` | `true` |
| `<list-table :hoverable="true" />` | `true` |
| `<list-table :hoverable="false" />` | `false` |
| `<list-table />` | `false` (default) |
